# Yolo Hall (`yolo`)

## Sources and inventory

- `csuc-floor-plans/Yolo/yolo-1.pdf` and `yolo-2.pdf`, both dated August 4, 2008. Native pages are 612 × 792 PDF points, rotation 0. Labels are vector outlines, so text extraction is empty.
- Both sheets print `1 inch = 40 feet`: `feetPerPdfPoint = 40 / 72`. Manual tracing used a 2-pixel-per-point rendering; raster coordinates must be divided by two first.
- `src/data/yolo/floor-1.json`: 142 nodes, 148 within-floor edges.
- `src/data/yolo/floor-2.json`: 125 nodes, 132 within-floor edges.
- `src/data/yolo/connections.json`: four adjacent-floor transfers, three stairs and one elevator.
- Requested `floor-1-preview.png` and `floor-2-preview.png` are source overlays generated by reversing the saved JSON transforms. They are local review artifacts, not application imports or commit artifacts.
- Sheets say 1 and 2 of 3; no third source sheet is supplied. No additional floor is inferred.

## Origin, scale, and alignment

The origin is `yolo-1-exit-south-ao`, the AO-marked exterior double doorway at the south lobby: native first-floor PDF point `(237, 637)` from the top left. Positive X is drawing-right and positive Y drawing-up; neither is a geographic direction.

Floor 1 uses `x = (pdfX - 237) * 40 / 72`, `y = (637 - pdfY) * 40 / 72`. Floor 2 has the same orientation and scale. Its outlines are translated approximately 14 PDF points left and 2 points up relative to floor 1. Apply `(pdfX + 14, pdfY + 2)` before the first-floor conversion. Its projected origin is `(223, 635)` in its own page coordinates.

Alignment landmarks include the elevator opening (floor-1 raster `(569,1126)`, floor-2 `(541,1122)`), the east office wing, and north stair outlines. Landing positions differ within stair cores and should not be forced onto identical coordinates. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal feet, not surveyed precision.

## Topology and access

- North, southwest, and east stairs connect floors 1–2. The elevator is a separate transfer chain. Transfer distances come exclusively from endpoint coordinates; elevator distance is zero after alignment. Stair costs represent horizontal landing separation, omitting vertical effort.
- The short stair above the elevator is shown only on floor 2. `yolo-2-stairs-above-elevator` records its visible access, but no first-floor flight, third floor, or transfer is invented.
- Floor 1 retains multiple entrances to 109, 112, 116, 118, 145, 146, 155, 162, and 167. Room 138 is accessed through 137, which opens into 136. Rooms 133 and 134 open from 132; 134 also opens into 136. Room 142 connects to the north door of 145. Room 144 has its own hall door.
- Rooms 110 and 111 are reached through 109 and 112 respectively. The service cluster 156/157/restroom/159/161 uses the actual openings and the narrow 162 circulation, with access from 150 and 166. Room 165 opens into 166. Rooms 174/175/176/177 are reached through 173.
- Floor 2 follows the west gallery and east corridor around open voids. No edge crosses the open-to-below regions. The 226 pocket is approached from the south of its void. Lab 224 routes skirt the west and south ends of shelving. Room 216 has a north door and a door into 218; 221 is reached through 218. Room 219 uses its north door from 218. Room 231 opens into 229, which has north and south doors. The west doorway between 234 and 235 is retained with a bend around casework. Rooms 245/246/247/248 open from 243; 244 has its own north corridor door.
- Unnumbered visible rooms retain spatial names. Roofs, furniture, and open voids are not destinations.
- Only source AO-marked exterior entrance approach edges are marked accessible. Stair transfers are false; other access is unknown. These drawings do not establish current permissions, elevator availability, or complete accessible routes.

## Validation

Manually inspected both source renderings and inverse-transform overlays, correcting void crossings, shelving approaches, and nested room access. Tests in `tests/unit/yoloGraph.test.ts` verify unique IDs, floor-local edges, finite coordinates, valid endpoints and kinds, no duplicate pairs or serialized distances, loaded two-way distances and attributes, four corresponding adjacent-floor transfers, independent loads, parent-room access, floor-filtered nearest candidates, and real Dijkstra reachability to every mapped node and back.

All mapped nodes form one connected building component after transfers. Remaining limitations are dated source plans, approximate manual coordinates, unknown present access, and the unmatched stair above the elevator. No GPS calibration or accessibility certification is claimed.
